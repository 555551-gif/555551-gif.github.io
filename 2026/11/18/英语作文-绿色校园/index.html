四级作文：How Students Can Contribute to a Green Campus

2024.12 · 四级作文 · 绿色环保

Recently, how students can contribute to a green campus has become a hot topic on campus. As a student, I believe we can make a difference through small daily actions. Here are some practical suggestions.

First, we can take part in speech contests on the theme of a green campus, which can help us share ideas on how to create a greener campus. Second, we should sort waste properly and encourage our classmates to join us. This can not only keep the campus clean but also reduce the workload of cleaners. Last but not least, we should stop throwing rubbish everywhere, because everyone is responsible for keeping the campus clean.

All in all, it is not difficult for us to contribute to a green campus. If everyone takes action from now on, we can create a better future together.
